How to play Pengui Doku

Use the rules together. Look closely, mark what you can rule out and work toward the solution.

Look for the most restricted region

Compare a colored region with the rows and columns it crosses. Work out which squares are still possible.

Mark squares you have ruled out

Use an X to record a square where a penguin cannot go. Keep the whole board in mind as you eliminate possibilities.

Place a penguin and check its neighbors

A placed penguin rules out the rest of its row, column and colored region, and all touching squares, including corners.

Use a hint when you need help

Hints can help you continue. Some hints and extra chances use rewarded ads, subject to availability.

A placement puzzle, with penguins

Fans of Queens-style placement puzzles will recognize the row, column and region constraints. Pengui Doku uses penguins rather than numbers, with no touching even diagonally. It is not a classic number Sudoku or a mine-finding game.
