About this penguin logic puzzle

What kind of puzzle is Pengui Doku?

Place a penguin in every row, column and colored region without letting penguins touch, even diagonally. Instead of filling in numbers, use the placement rules to work out where each penguin belongs.

Is it a Queens-style puzzle?

Yes. It uses row, column, colored-region and no-touch constraints together. Learn the rules and beginner solving tips.

Is it free, and does it contain ads?

The Android game is free to download and contains ads. Some hints and extra chances use rewarded ads, subject to availability.

How do I save my progress?

Choose Google sign-in in Settings to back up your progress and restore it on a compatible Android device. Online features require an internet connection.
